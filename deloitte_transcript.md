When a change is published in the government gazette, our CRS is updated. If the change impacts an act or rule mapped to your entity, the update automatically synchronizes into your instance within **T+2 days**. Both the assigned compliance owner and the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) receive an automated notification detailing the change, the revised due date or obligation, and the gazette reference. If a major legislative overhaul occurs—such as the implementation of the new Indian Labour Codes—it takes about one to one-and-a-half months to fully analyze, configure, and roll out the restructured compliance checklists.

### Part 4: ERP Integration, Access Controls & Workflow Architecture (18:30 – 24:45)

**Aries Senior Executive:** We run SAP Business One (SAP B1) and have our own internal ERP systems for data tracking. How does this tool integrate with SAP or custom ERPs?

**Prasad Nakashe:** Integration via APIs is fully supported. Our technical architecture allows RESTful API integration with SAP, Oracle, or proprietary ERPs. However, in our experience, over 95% of our enterprise clients choose to keep DCM as an independent compliance monitoring platform. 

The reason is simple: an ERP like SAP records financial accounting entries—such as the payment of tax or deduction of provident fund. But statutory compliance requires verifiable **proof of compliance**—the actual challan receipt, e-filing acknowledgment, Form 5A return copy, inspection log, or statutory register. An ERP typically does not store these operational and statutory proofs. In DCM, the compliance owner must upload the verification document and log the filing reference. Therefore, an API integration usually only passes payment numbers, which doesn't eliminate the statutory requirement to upload and audit the filing evidence.

**Aries Senior Executive:** Understood. What about user access control? For instance, can we restrict access so that an officer managing Indian operations sees only Indian compliance, or someone handling Provident Fund only accesses PF tasks, while the CCO sees everything?

**Prasad Nakashe:** Yes, access is strictly governed by role-based access control (RBAC) on a need-to-know basis:
* **Compliance Owner (Maker):** Access is limited strictly to the tasks assigned to them. A PF executive only sees PF tasks and cannot view environmental, corporate secretarial, or taxation items.
* **Reviewer (Checker):** Departmental heads (e.g., Head of HR) see all tasks within their functional and geographical jurisdiction.
* **Auditor:** Read-only access to inspect filed records and attached documentation.
* **Chief Compliance Officer (Admin/Super-User):** Full enterprise visibility across all entities, countries, departments, and compliance categories.

2. **Advance & Provision Tracking:** Often, an advance payment is made in Month 1, but the tax invoice arrives in Month 3. The Indian Income Tax Act requires tax deduction at the point of credit *or* payment, whichever is earlier. Tech TDS tracks advances against subsequent invoice bookings to ensure tax was deducted at the advance stage without deducting it a second time when the invoice is cleared.
